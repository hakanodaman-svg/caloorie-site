How it works

Logging a meal takes seconds, not minutes.

1

Snap, say or type

Photograph your plate, pick one from your gallery, or just say or write what you had — “two eggs and toast” works fine.

2

See the breakdown

Each item gets its own estimated weight, calories, protein, carbs and fat, added up into a clear total.

3

Stay on course

Watch your day fill up against your targets, log your weight weekly, and let your goal adjust as you go.

Features

More than a calorie counter.

AI photo analysis

Recognises every item on the plate and estimates portions, so mixed meals don’t need to be entered piece by piece.

Log by voice or text

No photo? Just say what you ate, or type it in your own words, and get the same calorie and macro breakdown.

Meal suggestions

Not sure what to eat next? Get ideas that fit what’s left of your calories and macros for the day.

A goal that adapts

Log your weight each week. Caloorie reads your trend and suggests a calorie goal that matches how your body is actually responding.

Processing score

See each food’s NOVA group, so you know not just how much you ate but how processed it was.

PDF reports

Export any day — or a longer period — as a clean PDF to share with your dietitian or keep for yourself.
